About Aldermoor

A university built on truth, discipline and excellence

Founded in 1894 on the banks of the river, Aldermoor University has grown from a single college into seven schools that share one campus. Students learn in small seminars, work in open laboratories and take their ideas into the community.

Our promise to every student is simple: we help you learn, prepare you to lead and encourage you to inspire others.

Admissions

Apply in four steps

Every application is read in full by two admissions officers. There is no minimum test score, and the application fee is waived for anyone who asks.

Open the application

  1. Create your profile

    Choose a program and upload your transcripts.

  2. Submit your materials

    Personal essay, two references and a short portfolio or project summary.

  3. Interview

    A 30-minute conversation with a faculty member, online or on campus.

  4. Receive your decision

    Decisions are posted to your profile; accepted students have 30 days to reply.
